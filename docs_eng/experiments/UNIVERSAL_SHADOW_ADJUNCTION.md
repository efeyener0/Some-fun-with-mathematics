# Universal Shadow Adjunction

`universal_shadow_adjunction.py` explicitly constructs the universal unital
nonassociative extension of

\[
A=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}\{1,h,q\},\qquad
h^2=q,\ hq=1,\ qh=-1,\ q^2=q
\]

obtained by adjoining only \(s^2=h\). It is infinite-dimensional. The
six-dimensional shadow clock is a surjective quotient with many extra
relations.

## Universal problem and tree construction

Objects are unital real nonassociative algebras \(B\), a unit-preserving
embedding \(j:A\hookrightarrow B\), and \(b\in B\) with \(b^2=j(h)\).
Morphisms preserve \(A\), the unit, and the chosen root.

Start from all ordered full binary trees over \(\{1,h,q,s\}\), with product
given by grafting, and quotient by the two-sided ideal generated by

\[
1x\to x,\quad x1\to x,\quad
hh\to q,\quad hq\to1,\quad qh\to-1,\quad qq\to q,\quad ss\to h.
\]

No reassociation is allowed. In particular,
\(L_s^2=L_h\), \(R_s^2=R_h\), \(sh=hs\), and \(sq=qs\) are not presentation
relations.

## Termination, confluence, and basis

Every rewrite reduces leaf count by one, proving termination. Critical-pair
analysis has one root peak, \(1\cdot1\), while reducible variable overlaps and
disjoint overlaps commute schematically. Hence local joinability plus
termination gives a unique signed irreducible normal form.

If \(N\) is linear normalization, every defining relation lies in
\(\ker N\), and every tree satisfies \(t-N(t)\in I\). Therefore

\[
I=\ker N,
\]

so irreducible trees form a genuine real vector-space basis. The distinct
singleton normal forms \(1,h,q\) prove that \(A\hookrightarrow U\) remains
injective.

## Infinite dimension and growth

For every \(n\), the left comb \(w_n=(((hs)s)\cdots s)\) is irreducible and
has a distinct leaf count. Thus \(\dim_{\mathbb R}U=\infty\).

Let \(b_n\) count irreducible trees of size \(n\) over \(h,q,s\). Then

\[
b_1=3,\quad b_2=4,\quad
b_n=\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}b_ib_{n-i}\quad(n\ge3).
\]

The first values are \(3,4,24,160,1152,8768,69504\), and

\[
B(z)=3z-5z^2+B(z)^2
=\frac{1-\sqrt{(1-2z)(1-10z)}}2.
\]

\[
b_n\sim\frac{10^n}{\sqrt{20\pi}\,n^{3/2}}.
\]

## Universal map

For any target \((B,j,b)\), recursively evaluate leaves as
\(1_B,j(h),j(q),b\) and internal nodes by the target product. All seven
relations evaluate to zero, so evaluation descends uniquely to

\[
\overline{\operatorname{ev}}:U\to B.
\]

This supplies the universal property explicitly.

## The six-dimensional clock is a quotient

The clock gives a surjection

\[
\boxed{U\twoheadrightarrow C}.
\]

It is not injective: \(hs\) and \(sh\) are distinct irreducible trees in
\(U\), but both evaluate to \(t\) in the clock. Likewise the regular-operator
contracts contribute additional kernel relations.

Clock-evaluation collapse by leaf size:

| Size | \(U\) basis trees | Clock images | Largest fibre | Zero image? |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|:---:|
| 1 | 4 | 4 | 1 | no |
| 2 | 4 | 2 | 2 | no |
| 3 | 24 | 7 | 8 | yes |
| 4 | 160 | 13 | 72 | yes |
| 5 | 1,152 | 13 | 608 | yes |
| 6 | 8,768 | 13 | 5,264 | yes |

From size four onward the image is
\(\{0,\pm1,\pm h,\pm q,\pm s,\pm t,\pm u\}\).

## Executable proof layers

```powershell
python experiments/universal_shadow_adjunction.py test
python experiments/universal_shadow_adjunction.py test --deep
```

Default mode exhausts 15,764 raw trees and 26,229 one-step branches through
size five. Deep mode exhausts 187,796 trees and 373,877 branches through size
six, and rejects a deliberately corrupted `ss -> q` rule by both presentation
fingerprint and independent clock semantics.

Bounded enumeration tests the implementation; the infinite confluence claim
comes from termination plus complete critical-overlap classification.

## Proof boundary

The presentation, normal-form basis, injected source algebra, universal map,
infinite dimension, growth series/asymptotics, and nontrivial quotient to the
clock are proved. Finite dimensionality, strong regular-root identities,
power-associativity, universality of the clock, classification of finite
quotients/ideals/representations, and a physical shadow-time coordinate are
not claimed.
