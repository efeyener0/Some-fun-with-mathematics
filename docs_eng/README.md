## Source algebra

The core of this toy box is the three-dimensional real unital nonassociative
algebra supplied by Misteria:

\[
A=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}\{1,h,q\}.
\]

Here \(1\) is a two-sided unit, and the defining products are

\[
h^2=q,\qquad hq=1,\qquad qh=-1,\qquad q^2=q.
\]

Multiplication is therefore neither commutative nor associative. A minimal
explicit associativity witness is

\[
(hh)h=qh=-1,\qquad h(hh)=hq=1,
\]

\[
[h,h,h]=(hh)h-h(hh)=-2.
\]

The same table carries a pronounced left/right chirality: \(q\) is a right
inverse of \(h\), whereas \(-q\) is a left inverse. The bracket-tree,
associator, operator-metaspace, shadow-root, and scheduler experiments study
different information surfaces generated by this small asymmetric core.

## The language of toys and games

Terms such as “toy,” “game,” “garden,” “weather,” and “clock” do not diminish
the work. They form its deliberate working metaphor. A **toy** is an executable
mathematical object with visible boundaries—something that can be turned,
broken, recombined, and tested. A **game** is structured exploration with
rules, moves, counterexamples, and surprising positions.

This intensive toy-like framing does not replace mathematical seriousness; it
makes abstract structures manipulable. The “garden” exposes Catalan
proliferation, “weather” exposes local state changes, the “seismograph”
registers fractures at projection seams, and the “clock” exposes chiral
operator cycles. Metaphor carries exploratory freedom; exact computation,
proof, and explicit epistemic boundaries carry the discipline of correctness.

This workspace is a collection of exact experiments grown around the three
original source files without modifying them. Its purpose is not to force a
single “correct product,” but to probe nonassociative algebra while keeping
its distinct information layers separate.

## Architectural separation

The original “path/history” intuition split into three authoritative
contracts:

| Layer | Preserves | Correctness role |
|---|---|---|
| Effect graph + serial oracle | Payload causality and permitted ordering | Scheduler safety |
| Operator word | Finite quotient of ordered left/right action | Control state |
| Tree + projection-seam ledger | Projection locations and local residuals | Path/contraction audit |

A nonzero associator need not imply a payload hazard, and a zero associator
does not certify safe reordering. Algebra and effect semantics are complementary
components, not replacements for one another.

## Strongest exact results

- The three nuclei and centre of the source algebra are \(\mathbb R1\);
  associator image has rank 3 and derivations vanish.
- Catalan tree maps are distinct through seven leaves, although evaluation on
  a fixed `h/q` word lands in only \(\{\pm1,\pm h,\pm q\}\).
- Rotation edges factor as
  \(\Delta_e^{root}=C_e(r_e)\); damped edges are nonzero residuals in a
  singular context kernel.
- A strong two-sided root \(s^2=h\) requires dimension at least six; one
  explicit completion generates a 384-state clock group.
- The ordinary universal adjunction imposing only \(s^2=h\) is
  infinite-dimensional, with the 6D clock as a non-injective quotient.
- The combined operator monoid has 192 states inside the ambient 216 signed
  transformations, generator rank 3, and exact image spectrum \(192,36,6\).
- Three root probes reconstruct the whole 192-state code; three rank-1 erasers
  induce the same kernel lattice in the opposite direction.
- Rank alone is not Markovian: exact minimization produces a five-state weather
  machine with mean \(11/2\) steps from identity to rank 1.
- The abstract generalization has
  \(|M_n|=(2n)^n-2^{n-1}n!\), but only \(n=3\) is identified with the source
  nonassociative algebra.

## Epistemic boundary

Finite counts, exact row reductions, closure scans, basis-map comparisons,
rewriting checks, and scheduler corpora described in the individual reports
were executed. Physical interpretations, GPU performance, all-degree Catalan
injectivity, minimal seam compression, full classification of six-dimensional
strong-root completions, and the representation theory of the universal
quotient remain open.

The central interface result is:

```text
payload truth        := explicit effects + serial semantics
ordered control      := finite operator quotient
bracket/path history := explicit tree + projection seams
```
